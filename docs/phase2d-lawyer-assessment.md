# Phase 2D lawyer assessment

Actual qualified-lawyer assessment, disagreement rate, Cohen's kappa, adjudication,
consensus and legal approval are **NOT RUN / NOT APPROVED**. No reviewer names,
licenses, attestations, signatures or assessments were fabricated. Test-only
fictional reviewer records exercise validation and never count as human results.

The replacement workflow supports PREPARED, ASSIGNED, PARTIALLY_REVIEWED,
INDEPENDENT_REVIEWS_COMPLETE, ADJUDICATION_PENDING, ADJUDICATED and INVALID_REVIEW.
NOT_RUN is retained independently for actual human assessment. A filled JSON file
alone cannot complete real review: it needs actual assignment, supplied origin/
qualification/independence/source-first attestations, full validated coverage,
finalization and separate coordinator confirmation of external submission origin.
Software cannot independently verify professional licenses or genuine independence;
that is an external operational responsibility, not inferred from booleans.

Each obligation/provision supports exact source identity/version/hash/spans, actor,
action/object/beneficiary, modality/polarity, conditions/exceptions/trigger/deadline,
documentary support, legal applicability, missing evidence, severity and explanation.
Use explicit/supported_by_definition/ambiguous/unknown/not_present/unsupported
labels as appropriate. Positive documentary labels require exact evidence.
An absent beneficiary may be correctly `not_present`, not automatically an error.
Approving an extracted role is not contract signature approval.

Two reviewers work independently and source-first, with separate outputs and
immutable submission histories. A finalized original cannot be replaced. Coverage
and disagreements are per case/provision/field. Per-field categorical agreement
reuses the existing function; degenerate kappa is undefined, never invented 1.0.
No global kappa pools unrelated actor names, legal support and severity.

Adjudicator identity must differ from both reviewers. Each disagreement needs a
bounded reason and valid source evidence. Original submissions remain bytewise
unchanged. The human-readable summary is this report plus the workflow status;
future coordinators should explain per-field disagreements and sample coverage
before unblinding. No actual disagreement data or statistic is available today.

Operational next steps: appoint two genuine appropriately qualified reviewers,
verify qualifications/independence externally, approve sanitized materials and
access rights, distribute source-first then blind candidates through separate
authorized channels, collect immutable actual submissions, confirm origin,
adjudicate disagreements, then explicitly unblind with recorded hashes. Do not
reuse the retired packet IDs/key or treat the visible engineering corpus as an
untouched independent study. No public review portal or external messages were sent.
